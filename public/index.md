# TextFixHub — Free Online Text Tools

> TextFixHub is a suite of free, browser-based text utilities: alphabetize lists, remove line breaks, count sentences and syllables, generate random words and creative prompts, and style text with Unicode. Every tool runs locally in your browser — no sign-up, no uploads, no server-side processing.

Canonical URL: https://www.textfixhub.com/

## Tools

- [Alphabetizer](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/alphabetizer.md): Sort any list alphabetically — instantly, with options to remove duplicates and reverse order.
- [Line Break Remover](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/line-break-remover.md): Remove broken line breaks from pasted text — clean up PDF and email copy-paste.
- [Sentence Counter](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/sentence-counter.md): Complete text statistics dashboard — count sentences, words, characters, paragraphs, reading time, and more.
- [Random Sentence Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/random-sentence-generator.md): Generate random sentences from a library of 500 hand-written sentences — no AI slop, no grammar errors.
- [Random Noun Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/random-noun-generator.md): Generate random English nouns from 2,228+ words — click to save and download as .txt. Perfect for games and brainstorming.
- [LinkedIn Text Formatter](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/linkedin-text-formatter.md): Format text for LinkedIn with bold, italic, strikethrough and more — 14 Unicode styles plus bullet and checklist lists. Free, no sign-up.
- [Fake Word Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/fake-word-generator.md): Generate invented words that sound real — Markov-powered with English, fantasy and sci-fi styles. Perfect for games, fiction and naming.
- [Syllable Counter](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/syllable-counter.md): Count syllables in poems and any text with per-line and per-word counts. Powered by a 20,000-word dictionary — built for haiku.
- [Random NFL Team Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/random-nfl-team-generator.md): Pick random NFL teams with one click — filter by AFC/NFC or division, no duplicates, team colors included. Perfect for fantasy and pools.
- [Word Combiner](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/word-combiner.md): Blend two words into new portmanteaus with syllable-aware merging — perfect for brand names, couple names and creative writing.
- [Mirror Text Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/mirror-text.md): Reverse, flip, and mirror text in three modes on one page — perfect for T-shirt prints, puzzles, social media and pranks.
- [Small Text Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/small-text-generator.md): Make tiny letters with small caps, superscript and subscript — perfect for social bios, math and chemistry. Free, no sign-up.
- [Wedding Hashtag Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/wedding-hashtag-generator.md): Generate wedding hashtags from your names — MrAndMrs, last-name mashups and classic phrases for Instagram. Free, no sign-up.
- [Incorrect Quote Generator](https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/incorrect-quote-generator.md): Generate fake conversations your characters never had — funny, romantic or angsty, with your own character names. Free, no AI, no sign-up.

## Why TextFixHub

All tools run entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your text never leaves your computer — nothing is uploaded to a server, stored in a database, or shared with third parties. Processing is instant because there are no server round-trips, and every tool works on large inputs in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
