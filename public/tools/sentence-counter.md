# Sentence Counter

> Complete text statistics dashboard — count sentences, words, characters, paragraphs, reading time, and more.

Canonical URL: https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/sentence-counter

## What it does

Sentence Counter is a free web tool at https://www.textfixhub.com/tools/sentence-counter. Paste your text into the input box, choose your options, and copy the result. All processing happens instantly in your browser — your text is never uploaded to a server.
